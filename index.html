About AMKO

AMKO is a specialized Electro-Mechanical Engineering company delivering end-to-end solutions for industrial maintenance and construction projects.

With a strong presence in industries such as steel, cement, and food & beverage, we offer tailored support for shutdowns, regular servicing, and complex installations.

At the heart of our operations is a commitment to international standards, environmental responsibility, and workplace safety.
